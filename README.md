# NeuroTraceR

A local-first, customisable workspace for creating printable tracing books, handwriting exercises, and extend with your own mindfull illustration decorations.

Print off multiple pages/cards of passage you like, then trace them out with your favourite fonts and let the ravens guide you through the nights Plutonian shore 🐦‍⬛🍁🎻🐧
